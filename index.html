8 · Registration form

Build the Google Form that collects your players. AuctionBook writes a script; Google creates the form in your own Drive. Nothing passes through us, and the questions are worded so the responses load back with no remapping.

  1. Open script.google.com → New project.
  2. Delete what's there, paste the script, press Run.
  3. Approve the permission prompt — it's asking to make a form in your Drive.
  4. Open View → Logs for the link to send your players.

Prefer clicking it together by hand? Use the plain question list instead. Either way, see the full guide — especially the bit about getting photos out of Drive.
